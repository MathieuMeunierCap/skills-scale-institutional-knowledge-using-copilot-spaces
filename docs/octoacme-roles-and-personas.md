# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Key Interactions
- **with QA Lead**: Collaborate on test strategy, accept testing feedback
- **with UX Designer**: Implement designs, provide technical feasibility input
- **with Release Manager**: Provide build status, coordinate deployment activities
- **with Business Analyst**: Clarify requirements, validate acceptance criteria

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Key Interactions
- **with UX Designer**: Partner on user research, design validation, and user feedback
- **with Business Analyst**: Collaborate on requirements analysis and solution design
- **with QA Lead**: Define acceptance criteria, review test plans
- **with Support Representative**: Gather customer feedback for prioritization

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Key Interactions
- **with Release Manager**: Coordinate release timelines and deployment schedules
- **with Support Representative**: Ensure awareness of customer impact and deployment communication
- **with Business Analyst**: Align on scope and manage project constraints

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers oversee the planning, coordination, and execution of software releases. They ensure readiness across all teams and manage the deployment process to minimize risk and downtime.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and schedule release windows in coordination with business needs
- Verify pre-release readiness (acceptance criteria, tests, security scans)
- Coordinate deployment activities across environments (staging, production)
- Execute deployment checklists and run post-deployment verifications
- Manage rollback procedures and incident response if issues occur
- Communicate release status and timelines to stakeholders
- Document release notes and known issues

### Goals
- Ensure reliable, low-risk deployments to production
- Minimize customer impact and downtime
- Maintain clear visibility into release status and health

### Typical Communication
- Release planning meetings with Product Manager and Project Manager
- Deployment coordination with Developers and DevOps
- Stakeholder updates on deployment status
- Post-release retrospectives and incident reports

### Key Interactions
- **with Developers**: Request build readiness, coordinate deployment activities
- **with QA Lead**: Verify testing completion, approve for deployment
- **with Project Manager**: Coordinate release timing and stakeholder communication
- **with Support Representative**: Brief on deployment details and customer communication plan

---

## UX Designer

### Role Summary
UX Designers focus on creating user-centric solutions that are intuitive, accessible, and delightful. They collaborate throughout the project lifecycle to ensure user needs are understood and addressed in the final product.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research and gather feedback on user needs and pain points
- Create design mockups, prototypes, and interaction patterns
- Validate design solutions through user testing and feedback
- Collaborate with Product Manager and Developers on design feasibility
- Ensure consistency with design system and accessibility standards
- Participate in retrospectives to gather feedback for design improvements

### Goals
- Deliver user-centric, intuitive products
- Reduce friction in user workflows
- Improve product adoption and satisfaction

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews and feedback sessions with Developers and Product Manager
- User research findings and insights shared with the team
- Design documentation and component specifications

### Key Interactions
- **with Product Manager**: Partner on user research, prioritize design improvements
- **with Developers**: Provide design specs, discuss technical feasibility
- **with QA Lead**: Ensure design acceptance criteria are testable
- **with Support Representative**: Gather user feedback on usability and pain points

---

## QA Lead

### Role Summary
QA Leads establish testing strategies, define quality standards, and coordinate manual and automated testing efforts. They ensure that deliverables meet acceptance criteria and quality expectations before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategies and approach for features and releases
- Establish acceptance criteria with Product Manager and Developers
- Coordinate manual and automated testing across the team
- Identify quality risks and escalate critical issues
- Validate that Definition of Done is met before items move to release
- Track test coverage and quality metrics
- Participate in planning to ensure testability of features

### Goals
- Ensure high-quality, reliable releases
- Reduce post-release defects and customer impact
- Improve test coverage and automation over time

### Typical Communication
- Test plans and acceptance criteria reviews
- Quality reports and metrics dashboards
- Escalation of critical issues and risks
- Post-release quality assessments

### Key Interactions
- **with Developers**: Clarify acceptance criteria, provide testing feedback, validate fixes
- **with Product Manager**: Define acceptance criteria and success metrics
- **with Release Manager**: Verify testing readiness before deployment
- **with UX Designer**: Ensure design acceptance criteria are measurable and testable

---

## Support Representative

### Role Summary
Support Representatives act as the voice of the customer within the development team. They gather user feedback, report issues, and ensure that customer needs and pain points are communicated to inform prioritization and improvements.

### Responsibilities
- Collect and triage customer feedback, issues, and feature requests
- Report production issues to the development team with clear context
- Provide context on customer impact and urgency of problems
- Communicate product updates and changes to customers
- Gather insights on user workflows and pain points
- Participate in feature validation and user acceptance testing

### Goals
- Ensure customer voice is heard in product decisions
- Minimize customer impact from product issues
- Improve customer satisfaction and product adoption

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback summaries and issue reports
- Escalations of critical production issues
- Product update communications and release notes
- Customer satisfaction and adoption metrics

### Key Interactions
- **with Product Manager**: Share customer feedback to inform prioritization
- **with Project Manager**: Alert to customer-impacting issues or delays
- **with Developers**: Provide context on reported issues and expected customer impact
- **with Release Manager**: Coordinate customer communication for releases

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts clarify requirements, analyze workflows, and identify process improvements. They bridge the gap between business needs and technical solutions, ensuring alignment and clear understanding across teams.

### Responsibilities
- Analyze business processes and identify improvement opportunities
- Clarify and document requirements from stakeholders
- Create requirement specifications and user stories with acceptance criteria
- Validate that proposed solutions address business needs
- Identify process inefficiencies and recommend improvements
- Participate in solution design and feasibility assessment

### Goals
- Ensure alignment between business needs and technical delivery
- Reduce rework and scope creep through clear requirements
- Identify continuous improvement opportunities

### Typical Communication
- Requirement specification documents and user stories
- Process flow diagrams and workflow analysis
- Stakeholder interviews and feedback summaries
- Solution validation reports

### Key Interactions
- **with Product Manager**: Collaborate on requirements analysis and prioritization
- **with Developers**: Clarify technical feasibility of proposed solutions
- **with Project Manager**: Assess scope and resource implications
- **with Support Representative**: Gather workflow insights from customer interactions
